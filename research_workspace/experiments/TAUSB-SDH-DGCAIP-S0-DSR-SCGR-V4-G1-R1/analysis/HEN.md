# TAUSB-SDH-DGCAIP v4 G1 H-E-N analysis

## Outcome

The guarded v4 G1 completed and passed all three registered decision layers:
runtime, mechanism validity and promotion. The controller observed a real GPU
process, exited with code 0 after 50.18 seconds and preserved the complete
eight-step trace plus the candidate state. Remote and local SHA256 values match
for all nine pulled files.

This is a surrogate mechanism qualification. It is not evidence that a fresh
victim trained on the materialized dataset will lose person AP50 while
preserving the other nineteen classes.

## H: hypotheses

1. The component-aligned route can make repeated target-directed updates while
   retaining non-empty protection nullspaces and finite gradients.
2. The v3 failure was mainly caused by promotion/accounting semantics rather
   than an inability to produce feasible protected updates.
3. The resulting candidate is sufficiently qualified to enter the separately
   scoped fresh-victim experiment.

## E: evidence

- Seven of eight updates were accepted: accepted ratio `0.875`, actual skip
  ratio `0.125`. Seven steps used backtracking, but successful smaller steps
  were no longer counted as skips.
- Every routed step reached target progress `0.60`; median attack retention was
  `0.483096` and remained diagnostic as preregistered.
- All accepted routes passed the safe-row and violated-row audits. Null
  dimensions remained between `3502` and `3514`.
- Frozen modules, replay slots, risk-bank binding and finite-state checks all
  passed. The candidate state SHA256 is
  `6574b876913b5d58dbc68f88c57f619a3c383bc6c7c1cf73c37f9891f561fdf8`.
- The aggregate non-target probability-drop proxy was small
  (`1.349846e-5`) and NLA macro loss was `3.883501e-4`.
- The perturbation respected `16/255`: measured Linf `0.0627448`, with zero
  support leakage.
- DLFC concentration was strong (median cosine `0.994502`). CICR direction
  concentration was substantially weaker (median `0.315388`, Q25 `0.046106`),
  despite full energy-floor pass and `93.73%` valid-instance coverage.
- P3/P4/P5 pooling coverage was `14.84%`, `34.85%`, `60.00%`; the low-resolution
  P3 branch is the weakest coverage point.

## N: next decision

The v4 G1 candidate is promoted to the victim-binding stage. Do not add another
surrogate mechanism gate before victim evaluation. Bind the exact candidate
hash into a fresh materialization/victim configuration, preserve the existing
C0 comparison, and report person plus all nineteen non-target AP50 values even
if the success thresholds fail.

Victim analysis must pay particular attention to the proxy outliers
`tvmonitor` (class 19, drop `0.02617`), `dog` (class 11, `0.01208`) and `sheep`
(class 16, `0.00973`). These proxy values do not equal AP50, but they show why
the nineteen per-class results cannot be replaced by the small macro average.

The current G1 also does not establish that the victim learned a shortcut. The
weak CICR cosine is retained as a mechanism risk rather than repaired before
the first real victim result.
